The Philosophy

Total Football, La Masia & Soci Ownership

Unlike franchise corporations, FC Barcelona is owned and democratically governed entirely by its supporters—the 144,000+ socis. From the revolutionary positional football laid down by Johan Cruyff to Pep Guardiola’s historic sextuple-winning machinery, Barça represents an unwavering belief in attacking creativity, possession dominance, and homegrown talent nurtured at La Masia.

Club History Timeline

From a humble newspaper advertisement placed in 1899 to the global powerhouse of today, step through the defining milestones of Blaugrana immortality.

1899

Foundation of FC Barcelona

Joan Gamper places a call in Los Deportes magazine to assemble a football club. On November 29, 1899, FC Barcelona is officially formed.

flag
Initial kit adopts the blue and deep red colors of FC Basel, establishing the Blaugrana identity.
Les Corts stadium opens in 1922 holding 30,000 passionate spectators, later expanded to 60,000.
stadium
1920s

First Golden Age & Les Corts

Led by the legendary striker Paulino Alcántara and goalkeeper Ricardo Zamora, Barça wins the very first Spanish League championship in 1929.

1957

Camp Nou Inauguration

With the sensational signing of Ladislao Kubala drawing massive crowds, the monumental Camp Nou stadium is opened on September 24, 1957.

domain
Europe's largest football sanctuary with a capacity exceeding 90,000 seats.
Ronald Koeman’s 111th-minute thunderous free kick secures the inaugural European Cup at Wembley.
workspace_premium
1992

Cruyff's Dream Team

Johan Cruyff architects a tactical revolution, guiding Barça to 4 consecutive La Liga titles and their first ever European Cup crown.

2009

The Unprecedented Sextuple

Under Pep Guardiola, Barça wins every possible competition: La Liga, Copa del Rey, Champions League, Spanish Super Cup, UEFA Super Cup, and Club World Cup.

stars
Lionel Messi wins his first Ballon d'Or as the Tiki-Taka philosophy captivates world football.
The 125th Anniversary and the reopening of the transformed Spotify Camp Nou stadium.
rocket_launch
